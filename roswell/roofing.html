Frequently asked questions

How much does a roof replacement cost in Georgia?

Most single-family replacements fall between $9,000 and $20,000 depending on size, pitch and material. Estimates are free and itemized line by line.

How do I know if I have hail damage?

Bruised shingles, granule loss and dented vents are the signs — often invisible from the ground. We provide free inspections with photo documentation you can take to your insurer.
