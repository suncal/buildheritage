Property Manager Renovation Quote Atlanta

Build Heritage offers Atlanta property managers a free, written, and itemized renovation quote. Submit the quote form or email info@buildheritage.org and you’ll hear back within one business day.

How the quote process works

  1. Open the quote form for Atlanta.
  2. Enter the property address, square footage, and the trades you need (painting, drywall, flooring, etc.).
  3. Submit the request.
  4. Within one business day you receive a PDF that breaks down labor and material costs for each selected trade.

What the quote includes

  • A written, itemized estimate for any of the eight trades Build Heritage offers: Packing & Moving, Painting, Drywall, Flooring & Vinyl, Framing, Doors, Window & Door Trim, Roofing & Tiles.
  • The 2026 cost guide (updated August 2026) shows typical ranges, for example a full roof replacement $9,000‑$20,000 or a whole‑home interior repaint $3,000‑$8,000.
  • The guide recommends holding back 10‑15 % of the quoted amount for hidden issues.
