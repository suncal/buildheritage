Georgia Home Renovation Quote

Yes, you can get a free, written and itemized quote for any residential renovation in Georgia. Fill out the quote form or email us and we’ll respond within one business day.

How it works for a single‑family home remodel

  1. Visit the Itemized Contractor Quote page and complete the short form with your address and the work you need.
  2. Our team reviews the details and prepares a written estimate that breaks down each trade—painting, drywall, flooring, framing, doors, window‑door trim, roofing, and packing & moving.
  3. You receive the quote by email within one business day, ready to compare with other options.

What you get, the cost guide range, and limits

  • Eight trades covered, so you can bundle painting, flooring, roofing, etc., in one quote.
  • The 2026 cost guide shows typical ranges: full roof replacement $9,000‑$20,000; whole‑home interior repaint $3,000‑$8,000.
  • The guide advises holding back 10‑15 % of the quoted amount for hidden issues.
  • Quotes are free, written, and itemized; no hidden fees.
