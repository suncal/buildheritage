Georgia Window Trim Installation

Build Heritage installs window trim for homes across Georgia. You can request a free, written, itemized quote and receive a response within one business day.

How the process works

  1. Complete the Itemized Contractor Quote form.
  2. A licensed and insured Build Heritage representative reviews your request and replies within one business day.
  3. You receive a detailed quote that breaks down labor, materials, and any finish options.
  4. Approve the quote, set a date, and the crew arrives to install the trim.

What you can expect

  • The work is performed by our Window & Door Trim trade, one of eight specialized trades we offer.
  • The 2026 cost guide (updated August 2026) shows interior trim projects typically range from $3,000‑$8,000 for a whole‑home job.
  • We recommend holding back 10‑15 % of the quoted amount to cover any hidden issues that may arise once work begins.

Service areas

We serve Atlanta and the surrounding cities of Marietta, Alpharetta, Roswell, Sandy Springs, Johns Creek, Duluth, Lawrenceville, Decatur, Smyrna, Kennesaw, Woodstock, Cumming, Gainesville, Athens, Augusta, Macon, Savannah, and Columbus.

FAQ

How fast will I get a quote? You will receive a written, itemized estimate within one business day after submitting the form.

What should I do if hidden damage is discovered? The cost guide advises reserving 10‑15 % of the quoted price for unexpected issues. Any extra work will be discussed and quoted before it proceeds.
