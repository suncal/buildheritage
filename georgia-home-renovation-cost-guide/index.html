Georgia Home Renovation Cost Guide

You’ll find typical price ranges for common home‑renovation projects in Georgia and learn how to request a free, written, itemized quote from Build Heritage.

How to get a free quote for a whole‑home interior repaint

  1. Open the Cost Guide page and locate the “Whole‑home interior repaint” range.
  2. Click the Itemized contractor quote link, fill in the short form with your address and square footage.
  3. Build Heritage replies within one business day with a free, written, itemized estimate.
  4. Add the recommended 10‑15% contingency for hidden issues and schedule the painting crew.

Typical cost ranges

  • Full interior repaint: $3,000‑$8,000 (August 2026 guide).
  • Full roof replacement: $9,000‑$20,000 (August 2026 guide).

Estimates include labor, materials, surface preparation, and cleanup for the listed trades.
