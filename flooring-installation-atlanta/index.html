Flooring Installation Atlanta

Need a new floor in your Atlanta home? Build Heritage installs hardwood, laminate, and vinyl flooring across metro Atlanta and Georgia. We give you a free, written, itemized quote and respond within one business day.

How a Flooring Project Works for an Atlanta Home

  1. Schedule a free quote – Fill out the form or email info@buildheritage.org.
  2. Site visit – Our crew measures your rooms and discusses material options.
  3. Receive an itemized quote – You see every line item and the total cost.
  4. Confirm and book – We lock in the date and start prep work.
  5. Installation – Our team installs the floor, cleans up, and hands you the finished room.

What You Get and How Much It Costs

  • Eight trades: flooring, drywall, painting, framing, doors, window & door trim, roofing, packing & moving.
  • Licensed and insured in Georgia.
  • Cost guide (August 2026): a full floor replacement ranges from $3,000 to $8,000.
  • Hold back 10‑15 % of the quote for hidden issues, as advised in the cost guide.
